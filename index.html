Since 1999

Twenty-seven years on Warren Street

  1. 1999

    Bastille Day opening

    Red Dot opens at 321 Warren Street as Raymakers, a Belgian frites, burgers and mussels bar. The mussels and frites are still on the menu.

  2. 2002

    Next door, and out back

    Red Dot takes over the building next door and adds a dance floor, a screening room and a garden with an outdoor fire.

  3. Now

    Dinner, brunch, parties

    Dinner five nights a week, brunch on weekends, and a long history of Halloween parties, fundraisers and weddings.
